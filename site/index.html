MAKE DELIBERATION PART OF THE JOB

Build a process
that earns your trust.

Set up a researcher, a reviewer, and an owner. Give each a written charter. Ask them to check sources, challenge assumptions, and bring you a decision you can review.

An example operating process you configure, not an automatic consensus feature. More thinking does not guarantee correctness.

  1. 01

    Researcher → evidence

    Collect sources. Separate facts from assumptions. Explain what is still unknown.

  2. 02

    Reviewer → challenge

    Check the evidence. Flag gaps and tradeoffs. Ask the researcher about its own work.

  3. 03

    Owner → decision

    Bring the findings together. Show the reasoning and leave consequential actions for approval.

OWN THE OPERATION

A problem you can
actually work on.

When an agent stalls, guessing is expensive. An inspectable coordinator gives you somewhere to start.

01 / INSPECT

Find the failure.

Look at task history, logs and the source path that made the call. Check whether the problem is the model, a login, a tool or your configuration.

02 / ADAPT

Change what matters.

Adjust instructions, select a different model, change task thinking, or patch the tool implementation. You can rebuild your own fork.

03 / OPERATE

Know the tradeoff.

You manage updates, uptime, backups and keys. Provider outages and model limits still apply. Self-hosting gives you control, not an uptime guarantee.

A few good questions.

Is it really free?

The software download and personal self-hosting are free under FSL-1.1-ALv2. AI providers, cloud computers, email and phone services have their own charges. The license restricts competing commercial hosted services.

Is the source open?

You can inspect, modify and self-host the downloaded coordinator under FSL-1.1-ALv2. This is fair-source licensing, with restrictions on competing commercial hosted services. Connected AI models and the optional upstream relay helper have their own terms and access requirements.

Will it always be cheaper or more reliable?

No. Total cost depends on your models, task volume, computers and integrations. You operate the coordinator and its updates. The advantage is control over the implementation and a way to diagnose problems; there is no measured reliability advantage or guaranteed saving.

Do I need a Mac?

The native app is built for Apple silicon Macs. Developers can also run the Next.js coordinator from source; Mac computer use and login services require macOS.

What do I need to get started?

Node.js 24+, Git, and your own AI provider key. For cloud computer tasks, connect an Orgo account and desktop. Mac tasks require a separate Codex setup. Optional integrations need their own accounts.

Is this affiliated with Grok or xAI?

No. Better Than GrokBot is an independent project based on Bops. “Better” refers to the control and ownership described above, not a measured speed or reliability benchmark. We are not endorsed by xAI, Grok, or the original Bops team.
